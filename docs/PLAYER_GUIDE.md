# Lvfe: The Xperience — a simple guide

This is the game in everyday language. No setup talk. No builder talk. If you can walk a street and use a phone map, you can play.

Play here: [https://iconiaglobal.com/lvfe/](https://iconiaglobal.com/lvfe/)

You can also add it to your phone’s home screen, or use the Android app. Sign in with Google if you want the same progress on more than one device.

---

## What kind of game is this?

Lvfe is a **street game**. The map is your real city. You walk to real buildings, shops, parks, and corners, then try to **own** them.

It is not a board you sit still for. Your phone’s location is how you “move.” If you are not near the place, you cannot take it.

Think of it as a mix of:

- a treasure hunt (find marks on the map)
- a land grab (put money on a place; highest bid keeps it)
- a neighbourhood team game (your side’s colour paints the map)

The seal on the splash screen is a stylized **Nigerian 1 naira coin**. The game’s money is called **NCN**.

---

## The one loop you need

1. Open the map and look around.
2. Tap a mark that looks open.
3. Press **Track** so the game draws a walking path and an orange circle around the place.
4. Walk until you are inside that circle (about **80 metres** — a short city block).
5. Take a **photo** of the real building the first time you claim it.
6. Spend **NCN** to **Claim** (if nobody owns it) or **Bid** (if someone else does).
7. Hold it, or come back later and bid more.

Short on coin? Open **Account → Wallet** and **Buy NCN** when that shop is open.

That is the whole game. Everything else is flavour around this loop.

## How to start

On first open you see the splash, then the **main menu**.

| Button | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Play Now** | Opens the live map. This is the game. |
| **Save** | Stores your progress. Sign in with Google so a new phone does not wipe you. |
| **Restart** | Starts over. Treat this as a hard reset. |
| **Check Catalog** | A list of places, same files you get when you tap a pin. |
| **How to play** | Short in-game rules. |
| **My places** | Places you own or have backed. |
| **Install app** | Put Lvfe on the home screen. |
| **Account** | You, your wallet, ranks, settings, sign in / sign out. |

Your picture (or a letter) sits at the **bottom left** of the map. Tap it for Account. Under it you will see your rank badge.

Turn **location** on. Without it, the map cannot tell if you are close enough to claim.

## Reading the map

Marks are real places in the world.

| What you see | What it means |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Red X** | Named place that is still open. Good first target. |
| **Black X** | Mystery / poorly known building. Often worth more. |
| **Circle** | Someone already owns it. Your own holds use a blue-ish circle; other people’s use their team colour. |
| **Green line** | The walking path after you press Track. |
| **Orange ring** | The claim zone. You must stand inside it to pay. |
| **You-dot** | That’s you. |

Search sits in a pill at the top, like a normal map app.

Three switches sit on the map:

- **SAT** — photo-from-above view of rooftops and streets.
- **3D** — tilts the city so buildings stand up.
- **AR** — opens the camera so nearby places float as labels in the street in front of you.

Location is a **button**, not a switch: tap it to jump the map to where you are.

Bus stops can appear as you zoom in. They are landmarks. You cannot claim a bus stop.

Banks and ATMs are landmarks too. You can find them. You cannot own them.

## The place file (the paper that opens when you tap a pin)

Tap a mark. A sheet slides up from the bottom — like a paper file. Peek first (you still see most of the map). Pull it up to read more. Swipe down or tap × to close.

Five tabs:

### Place
What this spot actually is. Type, quality, a photo if anyone has been there. Your own visit photo counts more than a stock picture, because you stood there.

### Mission
The briefing. Distance, walking time, cost in NCN, and the big verbs: **Track**, **Buy**, **Claim**. This is the “go do it” tab.

### Land
Who owns it, which neighbourhood colour it flies, and your notes: **Watch**, threats, **Plan takeover**. Collect 20 unique pins in a neighbourhood for a badge and a small NCN bonus — it is not a turf tax.

### Money
The desk: how much it costs to claim, what the place is worth, and check-in XP (you get all of it; the owner gets a 20% referral). No NCN is taken from a visit.

### Wallet
Your NCN on this phone. **Pay / Claim / Bid** stay locked until you are inside the orange ring with location on.

## Money (NCN)

NCN is the game’s coin. You do not “score points” in secret. **Whoever has put the most NCN on a place owns it.** If two people hit the same amount, the first one to get there keeps it.

Ways NCN moves:

- **Claim** — first stake on an open pin. Needs a ground photo.
- **Bid** — put more than the current owner to take the pin.
- **Top-up** — add more to a place you already hold. No new photo.
- **Check-in XP** — once per place per 24 hours. You get the full visit XP. The owner gets 20% referral XP. Walking through does not cost NCN.
- **Stars** — after a check-in, rate 1–5. High-rated places (4.5+) rise in search.

Buy more from **Wallet → Buy NCN** when buying is available.

## Teams (factions) and ranks

In **Enugu** you join one of four neighbourhood sides:

- Independence Layout (green)
- Coal Camp (red)
- Abakpa (blue)
- Emene (purple)

Your owned pins fly that colour. Pick a side that matches where you actually walk.

If you play in another city, the game builds local sides from real neighbourhood names there. Your chosen side stays with you when you travel; it does not silently reset.

**Ranks** grow as you stake coin and hold places:

1. Initiate
2. Scout
3. Pathfinder
4. Warden
5. Marshal
6. Sovereign

Inside a faction you may also show as:

- **Banner Lord** — top of that side
- **Vanguard** — near the top
- **Kin** — everyone else in the family

Ranks are badges. They do not replace the simple rule: highest stake owns the pin.

## AR versus 3D (they are not the same)

People mix these up.

- **3D** is still the map, tilted. Buildings look like boxes standing on the street. Good for “where is that block?”
- **AR** is the **live camera**. Labels sit on places in front of you, like looking through a window at the hunt. Close with × to go back to the map.

Use Track on the map to walk. Use AR when you are close and want to *see* the pin in the real street.

## Account, name, and not losing your game

- Pick a **username once**. After it locks, it stays yours.
- **Guest play** lives on that phone. Uninstall, clear data, or a new device can wipe it.
- **Sign in with Google**, then **Sync**, so the Android app and the website share one save.
- You can also **Export save** / **Import save** as a backup file.

Sign in does not use a separate “game email password.” Google is the door.

## What winning looks like

There is no final boss. You are doing well when:

- you hold pins you actually walk past
- your neighbourhood colour is thick on the map
- visitors pay you yield
- rivals have to bid hard to take your streets
- your rank badge climbs

You are doing poorly when you claim from the sofa (you can’t), leave pins with a tiny stake (anyone can bid past you), or wander through enemy ground without coin (tolls).

## A first afternoon (if you have never played)

1. Open Lvfe. Allow location. Allow camera when it asks.
2. Sign in with Google if you care about keeping progress.
3. Play Now.
4. Tap the location button so the map sits on you.
5. Find a **red X** within a short walk.
6. Track. Follow the green line. Wait for the “you’re close” ping.
7. When the orange ring has you inside it, take the photo and Claim.
8. Open **My places** and confirm it is yours.
9. Tomorrow, walk a different red X, or Bid on a circle you want.

## Common “is it broken?” moments

**Pay is grey.** You are not inside 80 metres, or location is off. Walk closer. Tap location.

**I took a photo and it vanished.** You closed the file or walked out of range before you confirmed Pay. First claim only saves the photo when Pay goes through.

**Black X looks scary.** It is a harder-to-name building, not a bug. Often a richer prize.

**I cannot claim a bank.** By design. Landmark only.

**AR is a black screen on some phones.** Use the map Track loop instead, or the Android app, which uses the phone camera behind the game.

**My progress disappeared after reinstall.** Guest save was only on the old install. Sign in + Sync, or Import a file you exported earlier.

**I am not in Enugu.** You can still play. Nearby named places load as you look around. Empty land still exists if that lookup is slow.

## In one sentence

Walk to a real place, prove you were there with a photo, put NCN on it, and keep it until someone puts more.
