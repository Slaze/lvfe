How to play

Play Menu Catalog My places Rules

Lvfe is a street game. Scout real pins. Track. Photo. Claim with NCN. Highest stake owns — no secret score.

Scout

GPS on. Red X = open named asset. Black X = unknown building (high value). Circles = owned. Green line = your Track route. Outside Enugu, Overpass pulls nearby named places; hinterland still works if Overpass is down.

Track → 80 m claim zone

Claim / Bid only inside 80 m. Tap Track for beep + green walk + orange pay ring. Approaching the ring? You’ll get a ping when you’re close.

Photo

First Claim needs a ground photo of the building. Confirming Pay saves it on this phone. Closing the file or failing the 80 m check discards it. Top-ups don’t need a new shot.

Claim with NCN

Open the place file → Wallet (or Mission Buy). Inside the ring: choose NCN and Claim. You’re backing the pin — not buying a sticker.

Who owns it

Highest NCN stake owns. Bid past them to take it. Ties favour whoever hit that amount first.

Factions & ranks

Pick a neighbourhood colour. Owned pins fly that colour. Dense ownership boosts neighbourhood value (up to +50%). Rank sigils: Initiate → Scout → Pathfinder → Warden → Marshal → Sovereign. Faction roles: Banner Lord, Vanguard, Kin.

Visit yield

Own a pin and someone else backs it → you earn ~10% visit interest (neighbourhood takes a cut). You don’t earn on your own coins.

Pass-by toll

Walk within 80 m of an enemy-owned pin and a toll may levy (max 2 NCN or 5% of their stake). Outpay to contest, Escape (fee) to refund, or Accept.

AR

Live camera labels for pins in front of you — street sighting, not the 3D map tilt. × returns to the map.

Catalog & My places

Same place file as the map (Place / Mission / Land / Money / Wallet). My places lists what you own or backed.

Banks

Banks and ATMs are landmarks only — no Claim.

Save & Sync

Guest play is local. Sign in with Google on APK or PWA, then Sync so both share one pack. Uninstall wipes local until you sync or Export.

One-loop cheat sheet

Scout → Track → walk into 80 m → photo → Claim / Bid with NCN → hold or Bid again. Buy NCN from Account → Wallet when short.
