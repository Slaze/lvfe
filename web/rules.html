How to play

Map My assets Catalog

Lvfe is a walk-around game in Enugu. You own a place by putting the most NairaCoin into it. There is no secret score. Whoever has put the most coins in, owns it.

Walk

Turn on GPS. Walk in the real city. Pins on the map are real shops, parks, markets, and buildings. The gold line is the walk to a place you marked. Follow it on the street, not as a video-game shortcut.

Get close — 80 metres

You can only put coins in when you are within 80 m of the pin. Farther than that, Pay stays locked. Tap Track to mark a place: you get a beep that speeds up as you near it, a gold walk line, and an orange 80 m ring around the pin.

Photo

The first time you back a place, take a photo of it (the building in front of you). That photo is how the place gets a real picture in your file. You do not need a new photo every time you add more coins.

Put NairaCoin into a place

Open the pin. Go to Wallet. When you are close enough, Pay is on. Choose how many NairaCoin to put in. Those coins go into that place. You are not buying a sticker. You are backing the place.

Who owns it

Whoever has put the most NairaCoin into that place owns it. If someone else later puts in more than you, they take it. If you put in more again, you take it back. Ties go to whoever got there first with that amount.

Neighbourhood (faction)

If you picked a neighbourhood when you set your name, places you own fly that neighbourhood’s colour. The neighbourhood inherits the place — it is “theirs” on the map while you own it. You can play with no neighbourhood; then the place stays Unclaimed even if you own it.

Visit interest (what you earn)

When you own a place and someone else walks up and puts coins in, you earn visit interest — about 10% of what they put in, paid to you from their payment. If the place has a neighbourhood, that neighbourhood gets a small cut of that interest. You do not earn interest on your own coins. Backing a place you do not own does not pay you; the current owner gets the interest until you overtake them.

Pay only when close

No remote capture. If you are not within 80 m, the game will not take your coins. Walk closer, then Pay.

Beep and Track

Track a pin from its file. The chip at the top shows how far you still have to walk. A beep (and a buzz on the phone) helps you without staring at the screen. Mute is on the chip. Stop drops the gold line.

AR

AR is the live camera with place labels in front of you. Use it when you are on the street and want to see which building is which. It is not the 3D map tilt. × returns to the map.

Catalog

Catalog is the same places as the map, as a list. Open a row to get the same place file (Place / Mission / Land / Money / Wallet). Default view is your places.

Banks cannot be owned

Banks and ATMs show on the map so you can find them. You cannot put coins in or own them. Everything else with a pin can be backed.

What this is not

This is not a step-counter that drips points for walking. Coins in a place are the whole game. Play on this phone — your name, your coins, your places live here.
