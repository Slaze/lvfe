How to play

Play Menu Catalog My places Rules

Lvfe is a walk-around game in Enugu. You own a place by putting the most NCN into it. There is no secret score. Whoever has put the most coins in, owns it.

Walk

Turn on GPS. Walk in the real city. Pins on the map are real shops, parks, markets, and buildings. Unclaimed named places are a red X. Unknown buildings (no name, quality D) are a black X and are worth the most. Owned places are circles. The green line is the walk to a place you marked. Follow it on the street, not as a video-game shortcut. Tap GPS and the map flies to you. Outside Enugu the bundled catalog is empty — the game pulls named places near your GPS from OpenStreetMap (Overpass), caches them, and still offers Unclaimed area hinterland if Overpass is down.

Get close — 80 metres

You can only put coins in when you are within 80 m of the pin. Farther than that, Pay stays locked. Tap Track to mark a place: you get a beep that speeds up as you near it, a green walk line, and an orange 80 m ring around the pin.

Photo

The first time you back a place, take a photo of it (the building in front of you). Pay confirms that photo onto this phone. Closing the place file, cancelling the camera, or failing the 80 m check discards it. You do not need a new photo every time you add more coins.

Put NCN into a place

Open the pin. Go to Wallet. When you are close enough, Pay is on. Choose how many NCN to put in. Those coins go into that place. You are not buying a sticker. You are backing the place.

Who owns it

Whoever has put the most NCN into that place owns it. If someone else later puts in more than you, they take it. If you put in more again, you take it back. Ties go to whoever got there first with that amount.

Neighbourhood (faction)

If you picked a neighbourhood when you set your name, places you own fly that neighbourhood’s colour. The neighbourhood inherits the place — it is “theirs” on the map while you own it. You can play with no neighbourhood; then the place stays Unclaimed even if you own it.

The neighbourhood with the most owned places makes every place there worth more (up to half again), so it costs more to back and pays more visit interest; hinterland outside named neighbourhoods gets no extra.

Visit interest (what you earn)

When you own a place and someone else walks up and puts coins in, you earn visit interest — about 10% of what they put in, paid to you from their payment. If the place has a neighbourhood, that neighbourhood gets a small cut of that interest. You do not earn interest on your own coins. Backing a place you do not own does not pay you; the current owner gets the interest until you overtake them.

Pay only when close

No remote capture. If you are not within 80 m, the game will not take your coins. Walk closer, then Pay.

Beep and Track

Track a pin from its file. The chip at the top shows how far you still have to walk. A beep (and a buzz on the phone) helps you without staring at the screen. Mute is on the chip. Stop drops the green line.

AR

AR is the live camera with place labels in front of you. Use it when you are on the street and want to see which building is which. It is not the 3D map tilt. × returns to the map.

Catalog

Catalog is the same places as the map, as a list. Open a row to get the same place file (Place / Mission / Land / Money / Wallet). Default view is your places.

Banks cannot be owned

Banks and ATMs show on the map so you can find them. You cannot put coins in or own them. Everything else with a pin can be backed.

Your record

Your name, neighbourhood, coins, visits, and place photos live on this device. Guest play stays local. Sign in with Google on the phone APK or the HTTPS PWA (`https://iconiaglobal.com/lvfe/`) to pull/push the same cloud save pack via `lvfe-save`. Uninstall still wipes local storage until you sync or Export save.

What this is not

This is not a step-counter that drips points for walking. Coins in a place are the whole game. Play as a guest on one device, or sign in with Google so APK and PWA share progress.
